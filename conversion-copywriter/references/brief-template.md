## Quick requests

**Generate ads:** “Use the brief. Create 4 distinctly positioned Meta Ads for [audience], each with hook, visual, primary text, headline and CTA. No invented proof.”

**Audit:** “Review this ad and its landing page as a conversion funnel. Score editorially, find the top 3 blockers, rewrite the hook/hero and propose a simple A/B test.”

**Video:** “Write a 20-second 9:16 ad for [offer], with timings, scene description, VO, exact on-screen copy, mobile readability and CTA.”
