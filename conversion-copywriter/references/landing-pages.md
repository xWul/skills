# Landing page playbook

## Main principle

Keep **message match**: a reader who clicked an ad should immediately see the product, benefit, and offer they expected. The page must answer, in plain language: What is it? Is it for me? Why trust it? What's included? What happens next?

## Suggested order (adapt to length and intent)

1. Above fold: specific promise; what the product is; concrete supporting line; clear CTA; helpful image or demonstration.
2. Pain/desired situation: articulate empathetically without fearmongering.
3. What's included: modules, deliverables, screenshots, formats, and uses as confirmed.
4. Benefits and method: feature → benefit → result framed within plausible limits.
5. Credibility: verified credentials, sample work, real testimonials, actual screenshots.
6. FAQ and objections: who it's for, prerequisites, access, support, returns, and conditions only if documented.
7. Offer and CTA: price, payment method, access details, and next step; no fabricated guarantees.

## UX notes

- One primary conversion goal and an intelligible CTA label.
- A hero action can jump to the offer or checkout; choose according to reader readiness and page length. Don't assume the ideal without testing.
- On mobile, use short blocks, meaningful headings, legible type, and visible buttons.
- Place video demos where they answer a likely objection; explain what the viewer will see.
- Do not bury an important refund condition, limitation, or qualification.

## Audit checklist

Check clarity in first viewport, ad promise continuity, audience relevance, offer transparency, proof authenticity, visual quality, page flow, anxiety/friction, CTA accessibility, and tracking instrumentation. Return high/medium/low-priority fixes with suggested replacement copy.
